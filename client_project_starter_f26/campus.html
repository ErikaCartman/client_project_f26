Campus Partner Resources

Working with Campus Partners

Campus partners play an important role in engaged learning. Students may work with organizations, departments, community groups, and other partners to address real-world information challenges.

Understanding a partner's goals, needs, and expectations can help students develop a productive and mutually beneficial working relationship.

Resources for Project Collaboration

Campus and community partners can provide valuable context, feedback, and expertise throughout an engaged learning project. Students should communicate clearly and establish expectations at the beginning of the collaboration.

Communication and Expectations

Discuss project goals, responsibilities, timelines, and communication methods with your partner. Clear expectations can help prevent misunderstandings and keep the project moving forward.

Building Mutually Beneficial Relationships

Engaged learning should create value for both students and project partners. Consider how your work can address the partner's needs while also supporting your learning goals.

Finding Additional Support

Students can use UMSI resources and communicate with the Engaged Learning Office when they need additional guidance during a project. Additional support may include templates, examples, project guidance, and opportunities to connect with campus and community organizations.
