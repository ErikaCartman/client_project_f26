During Your Experience

Managing Your Project

During an engaged learning experience, students apply their classroom knowledge to real-world challenges while working with project partners and teammates.

Projects can change as new needs, constraints, and insights emerge. Students should remain flexible and communicate regularly with their teammates and project partners.

Working with Your Team and Partner

Strong communication and collaboration are important parts of an engaged learning experience. Teams should establish clear responsibilities and keep project partners informed about progress and challenges.

Communication and Collaboration

Regular communication helps teams identify problems early, respond to feedback, and maintain productive relationships with their project partners.

Responding to Challenges

Unexpected challenges are a normal part of real-world projects. Use feedback, reassess your approach, and work with your team to identify practical solutions.

Reflect and Document Your Progress

Keep track of important decisions, project milestones, and lessons learned throughout the experience. Documentation can help you evaluate your work and communicate your contributions at the end of the project.
