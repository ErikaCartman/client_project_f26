Starting Your Experience

Begin with Shared Expectations

A strong project kickoff helps students and partners agree on what they will accomplish, how they will work together, and how they will respond when plans change.

Get Oriented and Learn the Context

Before the first partner meeting, become familiar with the people, places, and information that will shape the project. Use UMSI learning spaces to meet with teammates, review background materials, and prepare questions about the organization and its community.

Shared campus spaces give teams a place to meet, compare expectations, and organize their work before engaging with a partner.

Background research helps the team understand the project context and enter early conversations ready to listen and learn.

Project Kickoff Checklist

  • Confirm partner goals: Ask what success looks like for the organization and the people affected by the project.
  • Define the scope: Agree on realistic deliverables, boundaries, deadlines, and available resources.
  • Assign roles: Clarify each team member's responsibilities and identify the main partner contact.
  • Choose communication practices: Decide how often to meet, where to share files, and how quickly to respond.
  • Record next steps: Document decisions, owners, and due dates after the kickoff meeting.
